Terms of Service

Calvin Clip Studio is a personal software tool operated by Calvin Neumaier. It edits short gaming and entertainment videos and sends finished videos to the operator's own TikTok account as drafts.

Who can use it

The tool is used only by its operator for his own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

How it works

  • The tool connects to TikTok through the official TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API.
  • Videos are uploaded as drafts. Nothing is published until the account owner reviews and posts it in the TikTok app.
  • The account owner is responsible for making sure every posted video follows TikTok's Community Guidelines and respects the rights of others.

Disconnecting

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings, Security, Manage app permissions.

Changes

These terms may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.

Privacy Policy

What data is used

  • Basic TikTok profile information of the connected account (display name, avatar, open ID), used to confirm which account is connected.
  • An access token from TikTok, used only to upload drafts to that account.

What is not collected

No data from other TikTok users is collected. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer and are deleted when access is revoked.
